## Decisions

1. **No native app.** The app stays a PWA. App Store / Google Play accounts
   are not needed.
2. **No payment** in this app.
3. **Swedish is the priority language.** English as an extra language stays
   low priority.
4. **New optional feature:** admins can help a student regain access (new
   reset link or temporary password).
