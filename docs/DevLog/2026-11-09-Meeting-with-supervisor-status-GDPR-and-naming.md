# Meeting with supervisor: status, GDPR, naming and AI

- **Date:** 2026-11-09, 09:00
- **Attendees:** Klas Åkerskog, Fadi Alaraj, Md Abdus Sikdar, Maja Blom,
  Hans Malmefjäll
- **Absent:** Christian (project owner, Allez), Henric (course leader, Allez)

The meeting was planned with Allez for 2026-11-06 and moved to 2026-11-09.

## Topics discussed

### Status presentation

Hans presented what changed since 2026-10-23 (mock exam, practice, accounts,
admin area, statistics from the stored answers) and the MVP status: the MVP
and the next three parts of the plan (mock exam, statistics, admin area) work.

### User testing

The user testing starts after a presentation of the app to the users. The
development team is encouraged, but not required, to attend. Klas sends the
meeting invitation.

### GDPR

Registration asks students to accept a Terms of Use and a Privacy Policy that
didn't exist. Klas gave the team free hands to publish temporary texts, and the
team decides how long data is kept (Q-011).

### App name and wording

- Klas doesn't like the Swedish app name "Jägarexamen Tränare" and gave the
  team free hands to choose a new one.
- "Instrumentpanel" (Dashboard) is renamed to the common "Hem" (Home).
- The app's users are called "Deltagare" (Participants).
- "Öva" (Practise) in the menu is to be improved to something more common,
  such as "Övningar" or "Quiz".

### Mock exam

The "Introduction" subject is excluded from the mock exam.

### AI chat (Christian's idea)

Hans presented notes on what an AI chat could do and cost. Klas: an optional
feature if there is time. He agreed that since people increasingly have their
own AI subscriptions, it is not likely to add much to the app.

### Hosting

No news on the real deployment or who owns the hosting (Q-013).

## Decisions made

1. Temporary Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, written by the team; no approval
   step needed.
2. The team chooses a new Swedish name for the app.
3. "Instrumentpanel" becomes "Hem"; users are called "Deltagare"; "Öva" gets
   a more common name.
4. The "Introduction" subject is excluded from the mock exam.
5. The AI chat is an optional feature, low priority.
6. The team produces a questionnaire (form) for the user testers to fill in.
7. The user testing starts with a presentation of the app to the users;
   developers are welcome to attend.
